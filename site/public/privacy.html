Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026. Tailfuse is made by Ailio Technologies Inc.

The app

Tailfuse sends nothing to us. It has no account, no telemetry and no phone-home. License keys are checked on your machine against a public key built into the app. Your tailnet traffic goes through the tailscaled binary, which is Tailscale's software under Tailscale's terms.

This website

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. It is served by Cloudflare, which handles each request and its IP address to deliver the page. The fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives that request. Downloads come from GitHub Releases, so GitHub receives that one.

The license form

The email address you enter is written into your license key, which is how a key is tied to its owner. The site keeps no list of emails or keys.

A donation is optional. If you make one, the site emails your key to that address through Cloudflare's email service, and Stripe sends its own receipt. Stripe takes the payment on its own page, and your card details never reach us. Stripe keeps a record of the payment under its own privacy policy, and we can see that record in our Stripe account: your email address, the amount and the date.
